# Demo script (about 15 minutes)

**Link:** https://patguettler.github.io/potential-potato/
Everything runs in the browser. There's nothing to install and no cost, and every visitor gets their own copy of the data.
Before you present, click **Reset demo data** in the yellow banner so you start clean.

Switch roles any time with **Switch account** in the banner. Every account uses the password `demo1234`.

## 1. The curriculum (1 min, any account)
1. Open **Course catalog** in the sidebar: 44 courses and 408 lessons from Kindergarten through Grade 12, grouped into elementary, middle, and high school.
2. Click the grade buttons to show that **every grade has math, science, technology, and engineering**, with 100+ lessons in each subject.
3. Open **Python Programming**, **Principles of Engineering**, or **Grade 7 Life Science**: units in teaching order, each lesson with its length, question count, and written response.

## 2. Student: Alex, Grade 7 (3 min)
1. Sign in as **Student**. The dashboard shows 2 overdue assignments, what's due next, and **My courses** (the courses for Alex's classes).
2. Open **Photosynthesis and Cellular Respiration** (overdue). Point out the **learning goals**, **standards** (NGSS MS-LS1-6), and **vocabulary**.
3. Answer question 1 wrong first: it says "Not quite." Then pick the right answer and the **explanation** appears.
4. Finish the quiz and click **Mark lesson complete**. The score is first-try accuracy, so the wrong guess counts.
5. Answer the **written response** and submit it. It goes to Ms. Rivera's review queue.
6. Click **Next: Energy Flow in Ecosystems** to show course navigation.

## 3. Teacher: Ms. Rivera (5 min)
1. **Switch account → Teacher.** The dashboard shows classes tied to courses, a **Needs attention** list, and Alex's activity.
2. Open **Life Science Lab** to show the gradebook. Click **Export CSV**.
3. **Build a lesson** (the highlight):
   - Open **Course catalog → Grade 7 Math** and click **+ Lesson** on Unit 1.
   - Course, unit, subject, and grade are already filled in. Type a title and one objective.
   - Click **Start from a template** for the reading outline.
   - In the quiz, click **Multiple choice**, type a question and four choices, and watch the amber "Mark the correct choice" note. Click a circle and it turns green.
   - Click the 👁 eye to **try the question as a student**.
   - Click **Paste questions**, then **Fill in the example**: four questions of different types are parsed from plain text.
   - Click **Copy from a lesson** and pull in questions from an existing lesson.
   - The **Ready to teach?** checklist updates live. Click **Create lesson**: it appears in the unit.
4. **Assign a whole unit:** on any course page, click **Assign unit**, pick a class and a start date. Every lesson is scheduled with spaced due dates.
5. Open the **Review queue**, score a response, and write feedback.

## 4. Homeschool parent: Jennifer Johnson (5 min)
Jennifer runs *Johnson Family Academy* in Ohio (180 days and 900 hours required) for Alex (Grade 7), Emma (Grade 5), and Liam (Grade 10). She's also part of the Riverside Homeschool Co-op.
1. **Switch account → Homeschool parent.** The **Family dashboard** has one card per child (overdue work, scores, hours, up next) and what's coming up at co-op.
2. **My family → Add child:** add a child with a username and password. Children sign in on their own.
3. **Plan a course:** open **Course catalog → Engineering Explorers K–2** (or any level, since courses aren't locked to grade) and click **Add to my child's plan**. Pick a child, a start date, and a pace. Every lesson is scheduled on school days.
4. **Attendance & hours:** days and hours fill in automatically from lessons, plus manual entries (reading, PE, field trips). Show the progress bars toward 180 days and 900 hours, then log 2 hours of reading and click **Export CSV**.
5. **Transcripts:** switch to **Liam**. Algebra I and Biology are already on it, with credits, grades, and GPA. Add a course and watch the GPA update. Click **Print** for a clean transcript.
6. **Portfolios:** add a work sample for the annual review.
7. **Co-op classes:** sign a child up for a class with open seats. Out-of-grade classes are disabled. **Calendar** shows class days and field trips.

## 5. Admin (1 min)
1. **Switch account → Admin** (or **Pat (you)**). School-wide stats and activity.
2. **Users & families:** edit *Jennifer Johnson* to show linked children, then click **Create reset link**.

## Talking points
- **Real curriculum:** 408 original lessons (104 math, 104 science, 100 technology, 100 engineering) aligned to Common Core math, NGSS science, CSTA computer science, and NGSS engineering design standards. Every lesson has objectives, vocabulary, worked examples, and an auto-graded quiz with explanations.
- **Teachers can author like a school does:** courses → units → lessons, with a quiz builder that catches mistakes before students see them.
- **Free to run:** the demo is on GitHub Pages, and the real app runs on free tiers (see the README, "Free hosting").
- **Homeschool records built in:** attendance and hours toward state requirements, transcripts with GPA, and portfolios, filled in as children learn.
- **Co-op ready:** shared classes with seats, grade ranges, and fees, parent volunteer teachers, and a shared calendar.
